The Band Lineup

Meet the musicians behind Syth's signature sound and electric performances.

Lead Vocals

Xander Georgoulis

Commanding lead vocals with infectious energy, delivering Syth's hypnotic melodies and raw rock presence.

Guitar

Carlos Pena

Crafting heavy riffs, soaring leads, and dynamic textures that form the sonic backbone of Syth.

Bass

Maxwell Mitchell

Driving the deep grooves, punchy low-end baselines, and rhythmic foundation behind every performance.

Drums

Mark Jennings

The heartbeat of Syth, bringing relentless drive, explosive drum solos, and pinpoint tempo precision.
